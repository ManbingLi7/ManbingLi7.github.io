1Introduction

I measure cosmic rays with detectors in space, and I build the machine-learning models that read them. Models are trained on simulation but must work on flight data, so I treat every model like a measurement: it needs a calibration and an error budget.

Research interests: data-driven scientific simulation, deep learning for particle and detector physics, domain adaptation and simulation-to-reality transfer, and uncertainty quantification.

2AMS-02: Li and Be isotopes

PhD thesis in Prof. Samuel Ting's AMS-02 collaboration on the International Space Station: Measurement of Beryllium and Lithium Isotopic Compositions in Cosmic Rays with the AMS Experiment.

Isotopes of one element share a charge, so they are separated by mass, combining the tracker's rigidity with the velocity from time-of-flight and RICH:

m = Z e R / (γ β c)R: rigidity (magnet + silicon tracker) · β: velocity (TOF, RICH)
  • Multi-dimensional likelihood models of the mass distribution
  • Two-dimensional template fits for isotope yields
  • Monte Carlo calibration of detector response
  • Full systematic-error budget, reviewed by the collaboration

Try the ⁷Be / ¹⁰Be buttons on the AMS-02 model: same rigidity, different velocity, a smaller Cherenkov ring.

6HAMF with domain adaptation

Electron/proton separation for the TeV cosmic-ray electron spectrum, where protons outnumber electrons by about 100 to 1.

The gradient reversal layer makes the learned features unable to tell Monte Carlo from flight data, so a model trained on simulation generalises without retraining.

7AutoPhysics

A seven-agent LLM pipeline that writes, runs and improves Geant4 simulations on its own: planner, code generator, simulator, evaluator, optimizer, analyst and reporter, closed by an automated improvement loop. Submitted to BAAI Agent4S.
